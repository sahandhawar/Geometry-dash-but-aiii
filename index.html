Jump. Dodge. Don't crash.

A one-tap rhythm runner. Nine levels from Easy to Extreme Demon, a practice mode with checkpoints, and a beat that follows your run.

Click the game, then press Space, or tap to jump. Hold to keep jumping.

Controls

JumpSpace ↑ W, click, or tap. Hold to jump again on landing.
PauseTap the | | button or press Esc. Pick Practice or open the level menu.
Practice modeGreen diamond places a checkpoint (Z). The crossed-out one removes it (X).
Level menuUse the ☰ button, then the arrows or ← → to move between levels.
